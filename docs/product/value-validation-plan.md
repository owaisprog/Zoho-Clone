## Claim to test
An ISO operations team could identify the accountable owner and next action for a stalled merchant onboarding application more accurately and quickly using a shared tracking interface than its current system.

**Competing explanation:** Their existing merchant CRM/processor portal already solves this, or a tracking-only tool lacks must-have boarding/residual workflows.

## A. Message clarity and real pain
Seek **5–8 qualified people from separate companies** (target, not achieved). Include ops workers and budget owners. Before any demo, ask for one **recent actual** stalled application (without private details), tools used, who resolved it, time/cost and what worked in their incumbent product. Then show concept A/B in randomized order; ask participant to describe what it does/does not do.

**Proposed threshold:** At least 4/5 participants accurately understand the tracking-only offering; at least 3 distinct companies report recent material owner/action confusion. If they don't, reject/rewrite the message.

## B. Measured task comparison (only after prototype exists)
Provide five equivalent synthetic or legally authorized/redacted cases. Ask operator to locate **current owner, internal stage, next action** with their existing process, then a later clickable prototype; balance order to reduce practice bias. Record task time, correct answer, navigations, failures, and source of baseline.

**Candidate pilot threshold, not proven:** >=30% decrease in *measured* median time with correct critical states/ownership and no false external-approval implication. If no controlled baseline available, report **inconclusive**, not a guessed improvement.

## C. Commercial viability
Ask buyer which product/plan is used, what works today, what they would lose by switching, whether tracking-only is worth a **paid** pilot, which features are mandatory, required security/compliance, who authorizes and next concrete procurement action. Two buyers specifying real pilot conditions is a preliminary signal, not recurring revenue proof.

## D. Neutral sample questions
1. Walk through your most recent delayed application.
2. Who found the blocker, using which systems?
3. How do agents learn who is responsible now?
4. What does your existing software already do well?
5. What specific action would have prevented delay, if any?
6. After reading our concept, what would you expect it to do? What wouldn't it do?
7. Would it have value without boarding/residual payments? Why or why not?
8. What would make you refuse a pilot, even if the UI was good?
9. Who could authorize it and what data or security documents would they request?

## E. Private evidence and negative findings
Keep respondent identities, contacts, interview notes, recordings and actual merchant data **outside the public GitHub repo**. Store in an owner-controlled private system. Publish only sufficiently anonymized aggregated findings with consent. Log counterexamples, rejection and unknowns; never fabricate.
