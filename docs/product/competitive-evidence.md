## Price comparison - intentionally not treated as completed

NMI Merchant Central, ISOhub and PayHub need comparable verified account-specific commercial quotes. Public marketing pages alone cannot support a fair normalized cost comparison with self-serve CRM providers. Even published headline CRM list prices can omit onboarding, implementation, add-ons, integrations and user types.

The project must not claim our proposed prices are lower until comparing **the same functionality and organization profile**.

## Security / API verification - intentionally open

Marketing terms like 'secure', 'compliant', 'AI', 'integrated', or 'white label' are not equivalent to audited controls or access credentials. When choosing architecture or making contract claims, obtain applicable technical documentation, security reports, integration agreements and actual scope.

## Revalidation procedure

For each competitor update:
1. Record capture/review date and exact official URL.
2. Record product edition and whether feature is included, paid add-on or custom configuration.
3. Update matrix status **D/A/?** only with supporting evidence.
4. Note contradictions between pages and version changes.
5. Validate user-perceived gaps with prospects and hands-on authorized demonstrations.
