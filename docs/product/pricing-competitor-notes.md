# P0.08 — Price Comparison Scope / Interpretation Rules

**Status:** Public reference notes only, not equivalent quotes. All values checked against public vendor landing pages on 2026-11-13; vendor currency, promotions, limits, seat definitions and terms can change.

- **Zoho CRM US:** Standard $14/user/month annual; Professional $23; Enterprise $40. Source: https://www.zoho.com/en-us/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html .
- **HubSpot Sales Hub:** official sales pricing shows Starter promotional $7 and regular $20/seat/month, and Professional $90/$100 depending on commitment/promotional state; onboarding/add-ons apply. Source: https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales .
- **Salesforce Sales Suite:** Starter $25/user/month; Pro $100/user/month billed annually. Source: https://www.salesforce.com/sales/pricing/ .
- **NMI Merchant Central:** a payment-focused platform with onboarding, merchant/agent workflows and residual/reporting modules; no apples-to-apples verified quote available. Source: https://www.nmi.com/products/merchant-central/ .
- **ISOhub/other payment providers:** no independently verified comparable quote collected.

Do not compare a $14 horizontal CRM license with a complete ISO boarding/residual platform as if they supply equal workflow scope. For fair customer comparisons, collect at least 3 real customer-sized vendor quotations, including setup, users/agents, merchant portfolio cap, APIs, modules, support, onboarding, data export and contract term.

**Zero actual buyer pricing interviews have occurred**; no final list prices/discounts signed off.
