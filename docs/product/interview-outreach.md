# P0.04 — Interview Outreach and Follow-up Copy

**Status:** Draft. Not sent to anyone. Communications require product-owner approval and authorized use of connected services.
**Goal:** Invite targeted B2B operators/buyers for unbiased research; do **not** misrepresent interviews as customer commitments.

## Email — ISO / merchant services operator

**Subject:** Quick research question about merchant onboarding workflows

Hello,

I'm researching how merchant services teams track leads, agents and merchant onboarding across their existing tools. I'm hoping to understand what works well today and what still takes manual effort.

Would you be open to a 25–30 minute conversation about your team's process? This is a research interview, not a sales demo, and we won't need any sensitive merchant or payment data.

Thank you.

## Outreach rules

- Identify role relevance before writing; use truthful sender identity.
- No fabricated referrals, customer proof or claimed integration capabilities.
- Comply with applicable email/privacy rules and opt-out requests.
- Do not send mass unsolicited messages or scrape private contact data.
- Do not send actual messages unless the owner authorizes the communication.
