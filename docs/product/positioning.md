## Primary buyer and job to be done
**Priority #1:** Independent ISOs and merchant services companies. Economic buyer: owner/COO/VP Operations. Daily champion: onboarding manager, agent manager or sales operations lead. POS resellers are #2, PayFacs #3.

When an application gets stuck, the operations team needs to know **who owns the next step**, why it is pending and what can be done next. The first Zoho Clone application is a proposed **merchant-operations CRM** that links leads, agents, merchants, applications and staff tasks within one organization's restricted workspace. It tracks internal work; actual processor approval and payment processing remain with authorized external providers.

**Single-sentence concept:** "Know who owns the next step in merchant onboarding."

**Supporting line:** "Track the lead, agent, internal merchant application and next action together without replacing your processor's approval systems."

## One measurable outcome, not a generic all-in-one promise
**Primary metric:** Median time needed for an authorized operator to **correctly identify the owner, current internal status and next action** for a stalled merchant application. Compare current workflow against a later clickable prototype using equivalent redacted/synthetic scenarios.

**Proposed pilot target, NOT a result:** >=30% reduction in median time versus a *measured* baseline, with correct owner/status/action identified and no incorrect claim that internal progress means provider approval. Benchmark, cohort size, failures, and confidence must be disclosed before any marketing claim. **0 tests performed; no time savings demonstrated.**

Secondary signals: number of cross-tool lookups, % of applications with a named next action, time pending a handoff, and pilot buying interest. None are yet measured.

**Explicit exclusions:** No direct processor submission, underwriting, KYC/KYB verification, issuing merchant IDs, card processing, fund movement, residual payouts, terminal deployment, accounting, or complete Zoho-like suite at MVP launch. Future application tracks remain on the roadmap.

**Approved initial-data rule:** No raw bank/card/identity/KYC materials or credentials in general CRM; approved provider-hosted handling and minimum references if permitted. Design only; not an implemented certification.

## Competitive overlap and differentiators to test
Merchant Central and ISOhub already advertise onboarding/agent and related workflows; Zoho CRM, HubSpot and Salesforce have mature sales CRM. See [P0.03 evidence](competitive-capability-matrix.md). Therefore "the first unified merchant CRM" is not supported.

1. **Workflow clarity:** Does a team find assigned owner and next action faster than with its real current setup?
2. **Setup simplicity:** Can a small team configure its internal onboarding stages with less effort?
3. **Spreadsheet entry:** Can authorized historical data be mapped with fewer errors/manual handoffs?
4. **Narrow deployment:** Does a tracking-only internal layer offer paid value alongside existing processor tools?
5. **Future integration:** Are customers willing to expand later without promising current integrations?

Each is **unvalidated**. If existing systems solve the problem or buyers require direct boarding/residuals on day one, narrow or change the proposition rather than force a sale.
