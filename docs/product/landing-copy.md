# P0.07 — Landing Copy (Concept Only)
**Status:** DRAFT, NOT DEPLOYED. No public-facing brand approved. Product not yet functional or launched.

## Concept A — recommended initial positioning
**Eyebrow:** Merchant operations CRM • In development

**Headline:** Know who owns the next step in merchant onboarding.

**Subheadline:** Bring leads, agents, merchant applications and follow-up tasks into one internal workspace. Track what is pending and who should act next, while processor approvals remain with your authorized providers.

**Primary CTA:** Request a research walkthrough
**Secondary CTA:** Share your onboarding process

**Research note:** We're studying real workflows. No sensitive merchant or payment information is needed for a conversation.

### Planned workflow
**See the merchant context:** Associate a lead, organization, agent and application in one record.
**Assign the handoff:** Identify an operations owner and next follow-up task.
**Spot stalled work:** View pending internal steps and separately sourced processor status.
**Control visibility:** Restrict users to organizations and records they're authorized to view.

*All capabilities described above are proposed. They are not yet available to customers.*

### What it doesn't do
This initial concept doesn't process card payments, underwrite merchants, directly board them to processors or calculate residual payouts. It isn't a replacement for your processor portal.

### FAQ
**Can it submit applications to our processor?** Not in the initial concept. Any future integration requires provider agreements, secure APIs and validation.

**Can our agents use an external portal?** External portals are later roadmap features. The first product focuses on internal operations teams.

**Can it hold banking information or ID files?** Our initial design excludes raw bank/card/identity/KYC information from ordinary CRM records. Approved provider-hosted flows would be considered where required. No deployed security control is claimed yet.

**Is there a live trial or price?** Not yet. We are researching requirements and have not launched or finalized pricing.

**CTA:** Help us design the right merchant onboarding view.
