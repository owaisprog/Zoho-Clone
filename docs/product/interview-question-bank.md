# P0.04 — Neutral Interview Question Bank

**Status:** Draft for product-owner approval. Questions are tools for qualitative discovery, not a form to send indiscriminately. Select approximately 10–16 relevant questions per session.

## A. Company and role context

1. Tell me what your team is responsible for when a new merchant approaches your company.
2. Which parts of that process do you personally perform or supervise?
3. How is your team structured around sales, agent management, boarding and ongoing support?
4. Roughly how many applications does the company process in a typical month? Is that a measured number or estimate?
5. Are onboarding and merchant relationships managed in-house, by processors, or across several parties?

## B. Recent workflow and systems (most important)

6. Think of the most recent merchant application you worked on. What happened first, then next?
7. What systems did you use in each step and what did you have to copy manually?
8. Who was responsible for confirming completion and updating others?
9. Describe the last time the application could not move forward. Why did it stall?
10. How did you discover it was stalled? Who resolved it?
11. What is the longest or most error-prone handoff in this process?
12. If an agent asks for merchant status today, what steps do staff take?
13. Where do you currently keep agent/merchant assignments and history?
14. Are there steps that cannot be audited or reconstructed easily today?
15. How have you changed this workflow in the last six months?

## C. Cost, frequency and urgency

16. How many times did that problem occur recently? What period are you using?
17. How many people had to intervene? How much time did it take, if known?
18. Was there a missed deadline, merchant complaint, delayed activation, or financial consequence?
19. If the issue stays unchanged this year, what happens?
20. What operational problem did you spend money, headcount or project effort trying to fix most recently?
21. Which problems do you ignore because fixing them costs more than tolerating them?

## D. Existing tools and competitor coverage

22. What tools are in use for leads, agent management, boarding, residuals and ticketing?
23. Which tasks work exceptionally well in your current system?
24. Which paid features or integrations do you actually use, and which are unused?
25. What is currently customized or maintained manually?
26. If you tried to switch systems, what data and workflows would be difficult to migrate?
27. Are you subject to a processor agreement or vendor requirement that restricts replacement/integration?
28. When was the last evaluation or purchase of related software? What decided the outcome?
29. What are the most expensive or risky limitations of the current tools, if any?

## E. Buying process and commercial reality

30. Who owns the problem, and who approves spending on new software?
31. What is the purchasing process after an operations manager finds a tool?
32. What security, compliance, procurement, data-residency or contract conditions must be met?
33. Is there already a budget line for merchant operations/CRM tooling? No need to share a number if sensitive.
34. What comparable tool did you purchase most recently, and why did you choose it?
35. What would trigger a vendor evaluation in the next six months?
36. If there were a pilot, whose approval would be required and how long does it usually take?

## F. Specific buyer/champion probes

### ISO / merchant-services provider

37. What does an ISO agent hierarchy look like in real operations? Can a manager view subagents?
38. How are agent-to-merchant relationships recorded, reassigned and audited?
39. Which merchant application states are controlled by internal staff versus processor systems?
40. How does boarding status reach sales and agents today?
41. Are residual calculations and payouts handled by the same tool? Are they required in the first replacement?
42. What data would you permit in a tracking-only system and what must remain in the processor portal?

### POS reseller / provider

43. Where is the handoff between merchant approval, terminal order, technician scheduling, installation and activation?
44. Who tracks terminal serial numbers, shipment issues and multiple business locations?
45. Are reseller hierarchies different from sales agents?
46. What statuses are managed in the POS/processor platform versus your own tools?
47. Could a CRM/onboarding layer be useful before full inventory/deployment tracking is available?

### PayFac / software-led payments provider

48. Which portions of submerchant onboarding are controlled by your platform, external processor or compliance vendor?
49. What data is protected by KYC/KYB, AML, underwriting, risk or payment security requirements?
50. Which APIs and security reviews are mandatory for any new operational software?
51. What status visibility gaps exist outside your underwriting/processing core, if any?
52. Who has authority to run a safe pilot with synthetic data?

## G. Concept test (ask only after unprompted discovery)

53. What about a simple lead-agent-merchant application tracking workflow is useful, redundant or unsuitable?
54. Which proposed features match something you already have?
55. What would make a tracking-only MVP unacceptable?
56. Which single feature would you test in a pilot? What would success look like?
57. Would you approve a paid pilot if those conditions are met? Who must authorize it?
58. What would you remove from this idea?
59. What makes you skeptical of using an early product from a small vendor?
60. What evidence would change your mind?

## H. End-of-call verification

61. Let me summarize what I heard about your biggest friction point. Did I misunderstand anything?
62. Is the workflow we discussed common or a one-off incident?
63. Is there another responsible colleague who sees this differently?
64. May we contact you about a later prototype, using the contact channel you prefer?
65. Is there anything about your current system that we have not asked and should understand?

## Avoid leading questions

**Do not ask:** "Wouldn't an AI-powered all-in-one CRM save you money?"
**Use:** "How much time, if any, do people spend updating different systems today?"

**Do not ask:** "Is your current system too complicated?"
**Use:** "Which tasks were difficult during onboarding or setup? Which were easy?"

**Do not ask:** "Would you pay $399 for our solution?"
**Use:** "Who controls the budget, what was the last relevant purchase, and what would be needed for a paid pilot?"

**Do not ask:** "Do you want a better residual dashboard?"
**Use:** "Walk me through how residual statements are reviewed, challenged and reconciled today."
